Kebijakan Privasi

MGS Autopost Studio for TikTok hanya mengakses akun TikTok milik pengelola MGS Computer (@mgscomputer). Izin yang dipakai: informasi profil dasar (user.info.basic) untuk memastikan akun yang benar, serta unggah video (video.upload) dan, bila disetujui TikTok, posting video (video.publish) ke akun tersebut.

Aplikasi tidak mengumpulkan, menyimpan, menjual, atau membagikan data pengguna lain. Token akses disimpan terenkripsi di GitHub Secrets milik pengelola. Izin dapat dicabut kapan saja dari aplikasi TikTok: Pengaturan dan privasi > Keamanan > Izin aplikasi.

Privacy Policy

MGS Autopost Studio for TikTok only accesses the TikTok account owned by the operator of MGS Computer (@mgscomputer). Permissions used: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to confirm the correct account, plus video upload (video.upload) and, when approved by TikTok, video publishing (video.publish) to that account.

The application does not collect, store, sell, or share any other user's data. Access tokens are stored encrypted in the operator's GitHub Secrets. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy > Security > App permissions.
